The Problem

One in ten thousand molecules ever reaches a patient

Modern medicine has a discovery crisis. Despite record R&D budgets, the number of truly novel drugs approved each year has barely moved in two decades. Scientists are drowning in data — 250M known proteins, 10^60 possible drug-like molecules — but the tools to navigate that space are still rooted in 1990s chemistry and trial-and-error wet lab work. The result: patients with cancer, Alzheimer's, and rare diseases wait years for therapies that may never come.

The Bottleneck

Where drug discovery actually breaks

Target Identification

  • 3–5 years of literature review and assays to validate one protein target
  • Most targets fail downstream because biology was misunderstood
  • Genomic + proteomic data is fragmented across 100+ databases

Hit Discovery

  • High-throughput screening tests ~1M compounds per campaign — a rounding error vs 10^60 possible molecules
  • $50M–$200M per screening campaign
  • <1% hit rate, and most hits fail ADMET later

Lead Optimization

  • Medicinal chemists iterate manually — synthesis + assay loops take 2–6 weeks each
  • Toxicity and metabolism issues surface only after months of work
  • 90% of candidates die in preclinical or Phase I
Our Solution

An end-to-end AI platform that compresses 15 years into 18 months

🧬

1. Target with AI

TargetIQ uses ESM-2 and BioNeMo embeddings to rank disease-relevant proteins across 20K+ candidates in hours, not years.

⚗️

2. Generate, don't screen

MolGen designs novel, synthesizable molecules conditioned on the target pocket — replacing million-compound HTS libraries.

🧪

3. Pre-filter failures

ADMET Predict flags toxicity, solubility, and metabolism risks before a single milligram is synthesized.

🔗

4. Simulate binding

BindSim runs GPU-accelerated docking and free-energy calculations to confirm hits in-silico before wet-lab handoff.
