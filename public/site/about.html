MolexAI

MolexAI was born from a simple observation: the tools to cure cancer, Alzheimer's, and rare diseases already exist in the data. They're buried in 50 years of genomic research, protein databases, and failed trial reports — waiting to be found by a system smart enough to look.

Founded in 2024 by Kunal Singh and a team of computational biologists and AI engineers, MolexAI is building that system. We combine the latest advances in generative AI, protein foundation models, and NVIDIA's GPU-accelerated computing to compress drug discovery from decades to months.

We are not a pharma company. We are an AI infrastructure company for pharma. We build the tools that let scientists focus on what matters — the science — while our AI handles the search.

NVIDIA Technology

How we use NVIDIA today — and tomorrow

Today: MolexAI runs production workloads on NVIDIA H100 and A100 GPUs. Our protein and molecule models are built on NVIDIA BioNeMo foundation models (ESM-2, MolMIM, DiffDock). We deploy inference via NVIDIA NIM microservices for low-latency serving, accelerate molecular dynamics with NVIDIA Parabricks, and train every model on the CUDA + cuDNN stack.

Tomorrow: We are preparing our official application to the NVIDIA Inception Program in June 2026. Post-acceptance, we plan to deploy on NVIDIA DGX Cloud, integrate Clara for clinical-data workflows, adopt new BioNeMo models as they ship, and co-publish benchmarks with NVIDIA's healthcare team.
