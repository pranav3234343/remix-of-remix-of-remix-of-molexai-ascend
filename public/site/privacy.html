Privacy Policy

What data we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have.

MolexAI Inc. ("MolexAI", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have. Last updated: June 5, 2026.

Information we collect

Account data (name, work email, organization), usage data (API calls, model outputs, billing events), and any data you upload to your private workspace (protein sequences, compound libraries, assay results).

How we use it

To operate and improve the platform, provide customer support, send transactional emails, and meet our legal and contractual obligations. We do NOT train shared models on your private data unless you explicitly opt in to a federated program.

Data sharing

We share data only with sub-processors that power MolexAI (cloud hosting, payment processing, transactional email) under strict data-processing agreements. We never sell your data.

Your rights

You may access, correct, export, or delete your personal data at any time. Email hello@molexai.app and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest, SOC 2-ready controls, HIPAA-architecture options for Enterprise customers.

Retention

Account data is retained while your account is active. Uploaded research data is retained per your project settings — typically deleted within 30 days of project closure.
