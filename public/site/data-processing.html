Data Processing Addendum

How MolexAI processes personal data on behalf of customers under GDPR and India's DPDP Act.

This Data Processing Addendum (DPA) describes how MolexAI processes personal data on behalf of customers under GDPR, India's DPDP Act 2023, and similar frameworks. Last updated: June 5, 2026.

Roles

Customer is the data controller; MolexAI is the processor. We process data only on documented instructions from the customer.

Subject-matter and duration

Provision of the MolexAI platform, for the duration of the subscription plus any post-termination retention period elected by the customer.

Categories of data

Account identifiers, usage logs, and any personal data the customer chooses to upload (typically none in research workflows).

Sub-processors

Cloud hosting (AWS/GCP regions of the customer's choice), Stripe (billing), Postmark (email). A live list is available on request.

Security measures

TLS 1.3, AES-256 at rest, role-based access control, audit logs, annual penetration test, SOC 2 Type II in progress.

International transfers

Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) apply where data leaves the customer's chosen region.

Sub-processor changes

Customers are notified 30 days before any new sub-processor is added and may object in writing.
